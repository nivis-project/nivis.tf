## Scope

- Audiences, on `--surface` with a top and bottom border: an h2, two cards on
  `--ground` with title, muted lead and three bullets, then two code blocks
  side by side with a small mono filename label above each.
- Quick start at `id="start"`: h2 and lead, then four steps. Each step is a
  `52px minmax(0, 1fr)` grid with a 40px round warm number, a title, optional
  text at max 40em, a code block and an optional link.
- Step numbers are generated by the template, not stored in data.
